The maison

The art
of a trace.

ARS SHI began with a simple belief: a beautiful fragrance shouldn't be kept behind a glass counter. We compose Eaux de Parfum with the care of a heritage house, dress them in boxes worth keeping, and price them so you can wear one every day.

I

Composed

Each fragrance starts as a brief: a feeling, a place, a time of day. It is then built, revised and worn for weeks before it earns a name.

II

Dressed

Textured paper in deep fuchsia, forest, midnight, rose, cognac and taupe. Pressed with gold or silver foil and finished by hand.

III

Worn

Eau de Parfum concentration means fewer sprays and a longer trail. Two on the pulse points, one on the collar.
